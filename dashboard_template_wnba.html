Model Report Card

Every WNBA team, ranked by opponent-adjusted efficiency.

Built nightly from ESPN box scores and play-by-play. Adj O, Adj D, Net and Tempo come from an opponent-adjustment model computed in this page, so beating a good team counts for more than beating a bad one. Sort any column; Rk always shows position in the current sort. Games and Opponent rank re-window Net, AdjO, AdjD and Tempo (re-rated from just those games, with each opponent kept at its full-season strength), plus the shooting and four-factor columns and the record. Havoc, AST%, Luck, WAB, SOS and Radar stay season-long. Colors always rank the teams on screen.

Open any team for its game log, player stats, shot charts, lineups and on/off splits, and defense by position. Lineups above ranks every 2- to 5-player group in the league; Defense by Position shows what each team allows to opposing guards, forwards and centers. Search finds players as well as teams.

Every tab reflects the filter bar at the top, including Player(s) in/out, game type and rest days. Power Rank, Adj. Offense/Defense and Tempo are re-rated from the filtered games (each opponent keeps its full-season strength) and ranked against every team under the same game filters; small filters mean small samples. Luck, WAB, Havoc, Assist Rate and the tier record stay season-long. WAB's "bubble" is the playoff line (the average team ranked 7–9); the tier record splits games by opponent power rank. Team Profile ranks are recomputed live among all teams under the same filters (the Player(s) filter narrows this team's numbers only). In the Game Log, click a date for the box score (with quarter scores) or an opponent to open their page. ORtg/DRtg are per-game estimates from that game's box score. Shot charts, lineups, on/off and the assist network are rebuilt from play-by-play: lineups by walking every substitution (on-floor minutes match the official box score for every player-game), shots from ESPN's recorded locations. Clutch = last 5:00 of the 4th quarter or overtime with the score within 5. Shot-chart left/right are as drawn; ESPN doesn't document which sideline is which. Zone tables use the same 5-zone distance rules as before, not an official ESPN definition.
